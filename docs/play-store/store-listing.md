# Play Store listing: Note to Self

Drafts for Play Console → Grow → Store presence → Main store listing.
Character limits are Google's.

> **Name check:** several apps already use "Note to Self". Before you commit,
> search Play for the exact title. A distinctive subtitle in the title field
> (e.g. "Note to Self: Daily Reminders") helps both search and avoiding
> confusion.

```
Note to Self: Daily Wisdom
```

## Short description (max 80)

```
Your own words of wisdom, delivered at random moments through the day.
```

## Full description (max 4000)

```
Write down the things you want to remember: lessons learned, goals, quotes,
reminders to be kind to yourself. Note to Self sends them back to you at
random moments throughout the day, when you least expect them and most need
them.

WISDOM, ON SHUFFLE
• Add your own notes and choose how often you want to hear from yourself
• Notes arrive at random times within your active hours, never at 3am
• Give important notes more weight so they come up more often
• New notes get a boost so they show up while they're fresh

NAG ME
Some things need repeating. Turn on "Nag me" for any note and it will remind
you on a schedule you choose, all day, every day, until you turn it off.

RELIABLE
Reminders are scheduled days ahead with Android's alarm system and keep
arriving without you needing to open the app. If your phone's battery settings
could block reminders, the app tells you and takes you to the setting that
needs changing.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account. No ads. No tracking. Your notes never leave your phone.
```
