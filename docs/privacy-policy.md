# Privacy Policy for Note to Self

_Last updated: November 25, 2026_

Note to Self ("the app") is developed by Yox.

## Summary

Note to Self does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal information. Your
notes and settings stay on your device.

## Information the app stores

- **Your notes and settings.** These are stored only on your device, in the
  app's private storage. The developer has no access to them.
- **Scheduled reminders.** The app uses Android's notification and alarm
  system to deliver your notes. This happens entirely on your device.

## Information the app does not collect

The app has no accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash reporting.
It does not send your data to the developer or to any third party.

## Device backup

If you have Android backup turned on, Android may include your notes and
settings in your device backup, which Google stores in your Google account.
That lets your notes move with you to a new phone. This backup is controlled by
you and by Google, not by the developer. You can turn it off in your phone's
settings.

## Feedback emails

If you choose to tap "Send Feedback", your email app opens with a message
addressed to the developer. It includes basic diagnostics: app version, phone
model, Android version, and notification settings. Your notes are not
included. You can review or remove anything before sending. Feedback emails
are used only to respond to you and improve the app.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect
information from anyone.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data from your device. You can also use
Settings → Clear All Data inside the app.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with
